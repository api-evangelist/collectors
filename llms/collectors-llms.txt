# Collectors

> Collectors is the parent company behind PSA, PCGS, Beckett, SGC, Card Ladder and Collectors financial/vault services — the infrastructure powering trust, grading, authentication and marketplace/vault services for trading cards, coins, currency and memorabilia. PSA exposes a public developer API for certificate verification, order/submission tracking and population reports.

## APIs

- [PSA Public API](https://www.psacard.com/publicapi/documentation): Verify PSA certificates, track grading orders/submissions, and read population reports. Bearer-token (apiKey) auth. Base URL https://api.psacard.com/publicapi
